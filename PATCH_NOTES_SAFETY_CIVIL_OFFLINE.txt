Solar Audit Mobile - Safety/Civil photo and offline sync patch

Apply this patch by extracting it INTO the existing project root folder:
  SolarAuditMobile_ready/solar_audit/

Allow the archive to overwrite these application files:
  backend/app.py
  frontend/templates/audit_safety.html
  frontend/templates/audit_civil.html
  frontend/static/offline.html
  frontend/static/sw.js

Do NOT replace data/solar_audit.db, photos/, reports/, exports/, backups/, or your .venv.

Changes:
- Safety and Civil photos upload one-at-a-time immediately after capture.
- Pending online-page photos are kept in IndexedDB and retried when the connection returns or the section is reopened.
- Offline Safety/Civil photos use compact Windows-safe storage paths and deterministic retry IDs.
- Offline audit payloads no longer collect or apply NC severity, responsibility, target date, or observation details during checkpoint entry.
- Safety and Civil forms have a visible Review & Submit Audit button at the bottom as well as in the header.
- Service worker cache bumped to refresh the offline PWA shell.
